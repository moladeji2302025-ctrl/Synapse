## How to cite (APA 7th edition — confirm the edition with the lecturer)

- **Start of a sentence:** author outside, year in brackets — Sarker (2019) found that...
- **Middle of a sentence:** either form — ...as Eagle and Pentland (2006) showed... or ...(Eagle & Pentland, 2006)...
- **End of a sentence:** everything in brackets — ...lowered their grades (Lepp et al., 2014).
- **Three or more authors:** first author + "et al." every time — Lepp et al. (2014).
- **Several sources at once:** alphabetical, separated by semicolons — (Eagle & Pentland, 2006; Lepp et al., 2014; Sarker, 2019).
- **Place each citation close to the claim it supports.**
- **Reference list:** every author up to 20, surname first, "&" before the last — exactly as below.
