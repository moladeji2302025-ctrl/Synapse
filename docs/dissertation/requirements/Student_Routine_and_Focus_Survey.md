# Student Daily Routine and Focus Survey

*Requirements-elicitation questionnaire. Version 1, 2026-11-25. For Google Forms or paper.*

---

## Researcher notes (do not show to respondents)

- **Purpose:** gather requirements for the proposed system and describe students' everyday
  executive-function difficulties (time, memory for intentions, starting and finishing tasks,
  attention, organisation). These can come from phone habits, sleep, stress, anxiety, depression,
  ADHD and other causes. The form needs no diagnosis and names no condition.
- **Section B is not a diagnostic test and is not validated.** Use it to describe difficulties, set
  requirements and compare results between students with more and fewer difficulties. For a
  validated before-and-after measure, use the **ADEXI** (Holst & Thorell, 2018). If an
  ADHD-symptom subgroup is wanted, add the **WHO ASRS-5** (Üstün et al., 2017), copied exactly from
  the official form with its published scoring. Rewording validated items breaks the validation.
- **Never tell a respondent they have, or may have, ADHD.** Everyone sees the same closing
  message, which points to the university counselling unit.
- **Data:** answers about focus and difficulties may touch on health, so treat them as *sensitive
  personal data* under the NDPA 2023. Keep the survey anonymous and collect contact details for the study on a
  separate form.
- **Scoring Section B (researcher only):** Never = 0, Rarely = 1, Sometimes = 2, Often = 3,
  Very often = 4. Report each item and the domain averages:
  - time: B1, B2, B13;
  - memory: B3, B12;
  - starting and finishing: B4, B9;
  - attention and distraction: B5, B6, B11;
  - organisation: B7, B8;
  - restlessness: B10, B14.

## Introduction and consent (shown first)

Thank you for taking part. This survey asks how you plan your day, keep track of time, stay
focused and use your phone and computer. The answers will help design a system that learns
students' daily routines, plans their day and handles routine tasks for them.

- It takes about 10 minutes.
- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time and skip any question.
- The survey is anonymous. Do not write your name anywhere.
- This is not a medical or psychological test. There are no right or wrong answers.
- Answers are used only for this final-year project and are handled in line with the Nigeria
  Data Protection Act 2023.
